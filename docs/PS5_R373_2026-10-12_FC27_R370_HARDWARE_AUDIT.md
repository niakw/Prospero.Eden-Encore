## Next fix target, without a new user test

Prioritize reducing GPU-side duration of buffer_cache.mutex around the entire RasterizerVulkan::PrepareDraw/GraphicsPipeline::Configure. Splitting the lock arbitrarily is NOT safe: BufferCache, TextureCache, descriptor views, guest dirty ranges, GPU resource lifetimes and DrawIndirect depend on shared state and fences. Source audit or refactoring must establish the exact operations safe to preflight/prepare before both locks. The R364 and R370 commits mostly added diagnostics rather than a general hotpath speedup; R363 rare full descriptor ring optimization NEVER triggered here. Do not claim this run proves a performance improvement or that only one component is causal.
